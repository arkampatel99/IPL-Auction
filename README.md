# IPL Auction Room

A fan-made IPL auction game for phone and laptop. Pick a franchise, keep or release your players,
then bid against 9 computer teams or your friends for real IPL players.

## Features

- **Real players:** 703 players with stats from the IPL, T20 internationals and 9 franchise leagues.
- **Two formats:** a mega auction (₹120 Cr, retention slabs, RTM cards) and a mini auction built on the
  official TATA IPL 2026 list (350 players, 42 sets, real team purses, release your players first).
- **Bots:** Easy, Medium and Hard. They value players by stats, IPL experience, star power and squad needs,
  and prefer their own former players.
- **Play with Friends:** create a room, share the link or code, and bid live together.
- **Live auctioneer:** calls every bid, talks about each player's numbers, "going once, going twice".
- **Squad ratings:** every squad rated out of 100 from the quality of its players, with one clear winner.
- **Auto-bid, team songs, sold celebrations, dark and light themes, works offline after loading.**

## Quick start

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

See [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) for data refresh, songs and photos, and deploying to Vercel.
